MRPL — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE SOP-SAFE-002
Title: Energy Isolation / Lock-Out Tag-Out (LOTO)
Effective: 01-Jan-2026 · Owner: HSSE Department · Classification: INTERNAL

1. Before maintenance on any rotating equipment, all energy sources
   (electrical, hydraulic, stored pressure) must be isolated.
2. The isolating authority applies a personal lock; the executing
   technician applies a second lock. Keys remain with the individuals.
3. Try-start verification is mandatory after isolation.
4. Isolation register entry required: equipment tag, energy sources,
   lock numbers, names, date-time.
5. Removal of isolation only by the persons who applied it, after
   job-completion sign-off and guard reinstatement check.
6. Violation of LOTO is a red-category safety event and must be
   reported to HSSE within 4 hours.
