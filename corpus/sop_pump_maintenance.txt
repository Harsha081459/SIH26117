MRPL — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE SOP-MECH-041
Title: Centrifugal Pump P-201 Quarterly Maintenance and Inspection
Effective: 01-Apr-2026 · Owner: Mechanical Maintenance Section · Classification: INTERNAL

1. PURPOSE
Ensure continued safe operation of crude-transfer pump P-201 by quarterly
inspection of bearings, seals, alignment and vibration signature.

2. SCOPE
Applies to all centrifugal pumps in the crude distillation unit (CDU-2).

3. SAFETY
- Follow Energy Isolation procedure (LOTO) per SOP-SAFE-002 before any work.
- Hot-work permit required for any welding within 15 m of the pump bay.
- Confined-space entry into the sump requires gas test per SOP-SAFE-014.

4. INSPECTION CHECKLIST (quarterly)
a) Bearing temperature — record at DE and NDE; alert above 75 C.
b) Vibration — measure at 3 axes; alert above 4.5 mm/s RMS.
c) Mechanical seal — check leakage; >10 drops/min triggers seal replacement.
d) Coupling alignment — laser check; tolerance 0.05 mm.
e) Lube oil — sample for water content and particle count.

5. REPORTING
Findings recorded in Form MECH-F-12 and filed with the reliability engineer
within 48 hours. Any red-flag item requires an approval note to the
Maintenance Manager before the pump is returned to service.
